Privacy Policy

Zonerunner · Last updated: 2026-11-07

In short: Zonerunner does not collect, store or share any personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. Zonerunner (the "App") does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Data on your device

Anything the App needs to function stays on your device and is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the App removes it.

3. Third parties

The developer does not share data with third parties, because no data is collected. If your device, operating system or app store collects information when you download or use apps, that is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Children

Because the App collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the date above showing when it last changed. If the App ever begins to collect information, this policy will be updated first.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via github.com/larsfagerbakke. See also the Terms of Use.
